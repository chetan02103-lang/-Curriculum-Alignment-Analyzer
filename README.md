## 📌 Overview

**Curriculum Alignment Analyzer** is a web-based tool designed to help analyze academic curricula and identify how well course content aligns with important skills, learning objectives, and industry requirements.

Instead of manually comparing subjects, topics, and technical skills, the platform aims to make curriculum analysis more organized, efficient, and easier to understand.

Its goal is to help students, educators, and institutions identify missing skills, recognize strengths, and discover opportunities to improve learning outcomes.

---

## ✨ Features

- 📚 **Curriculum Analysis** – Organize and evaluate curriculum information.
- 🎯 **Skill Gap Identification** – Identify potentially missing or underrepresented skills.
- 💻 **Technical Skill Mapping** – Compare curriculum topics with skills such as Python, Java, SQL, MongoDB, Machine Learning, Cloud Computing, DevOps, Docker, Git, DSA, and Operating Systems.
- 📊 **Alignment Insights** – Present curriculum coverage and potential areas for improvement.
- 🔍 **Subject and Topic Evaluation** – Review individual subjects and their relevance to desired skills.
- 📈 **Improvement Recommendations** – Help identify topics that may need additional coverage.
- 🖥️ **Interactive Web Interface** – Make curriculum analysis easier to navigate and understand.

## 🎯 Problem Solved

Academic curricula can contain gaps between what students learn and the technical skills expected in real-world roles.

Common challenges include:

- Difficulty identifying missing technical skills.
- Time-consuming manual comparisons of subjects and learning outcomes.
- Limited visibility into curriculum strengths and weaknesses.
- Difficulty identifying topics that may require additional coverage.
- Challenges in keeping academic learning aligned with changing industry expectations.

👉 **Curriculum Alignment Analyzer aims to simplify this process by bringing curriculum evaluation and skill-gap analysis into one platform.**

---

## 🚀 How It Works

1. Open the Curriculum Alignment Analyzer.
2. Provide or select the curriculum information supported by the application.
3. Evaluate the relevant subjects, topics, or skills.
4. Review the resulting curriculum analysis.
5. Identify potential gaps and areas for improvement.
6. Use the insights to guide curriculum enhancement and skill development.

---

## 💡 Example Use Case

Suppose a computer science curriculum includes programming fundamentals, database management, and operating systems.

A student or educator wants to evaluate its coverage of industry-relevant skills, including Python, SQL, Machine Learning, Cloud Computing, Docker, and DevOps.

The analyzer can help organize the comparison and highlight areas that may deserve further attention, depending on the features implemented in the application.
